probe mods loaded through `--plugin-dir`, in tmux, subagents on `haiku` at effort `low` unless
stated. The probes and their raw logs are not in this repository; this page keeps the facts and
what each one decided. [SPEC.md](../SPEC.md) marks every fact that rests on one of these as
*verified*.
